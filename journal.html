Editorial Standard

Clarity, rigor, and public usefulness

Journal contributions should sharpen understanding, document credible experience, respect affected communities, and make knowledge more useful to institutions and the public.

  • Connected to a Society mission
  • Grounded in evidence or clearly identified experience
  • Written for thoughtful public use
  • Respectful of confidentiality and institutional trust
  • Transparent about perspective and limitations

What belongs in the Journal

Different forms for different kinds of knowledge

The Journal distinguishes argument, research, direct experience, institutional synthesis, and impact reporting so readers can understand the basis and intended use of each contribution.

Ideas and inquiry

Essays and research notes develop arguments, questions, methods, or emerging findings while making evidence and uncertainty visible.

Knowledge from practice

Field reports and Fellow stories document context, decisions, collaboration, limits, and lessons without turning one experience into a universal claim.

Knowledge for institutions

Briefings and impact reports organize evidence around a decision, operating question, contribution, or need for accountable learning.

Editorial review

Trust is built before publication

Review considers whether a piece has a clear purpose, a sound basis, appropriate permissions, fair treatment of affected people, and language proportionate to what the author can actually support.

  • Claims are distinguishable from interpretation and recommendation
  • Sources, methods, and material limitations are visible
  • Confidential or sensitive details are handled responsibly
  • Institutional and community contributions are represented fairly
  • The finished work offers readers a useful next question or practice
A public record that compounds

Publication is part of service

The Journal gives the Society a place to preserve hard-won learning across appointments and missions. Its purpose is not to manufacture activity or prestige, but to make credible knowledge available to people who can question it, adapt it, and carry it into further work.
