Start with the work

Find a fellowship route that fits

If you are new to fellowships, begin with the kind of contribution you want to make rather than with a title. These routes are orientation points; selection, host requirements, and appointment terms remain separate.

A question to investigate

Choose International R&D Scholar when your primary contribution is research or proposal development organized around a clear question. Public-policy research, procurement or contract analysis, and strategic-initiative research can be thematic directions when anchored in that question. This route is voluntary, part-time, and self-directed; it does not promise employment, compensation, visa sponsorship, clearance, or funding.

Explore International R&D Scholars

A defined host-based role

Choose a host-based field fellowship or appointment when the work is operational or supervised—such as contract management, public administration, program delivery, implementation, or service. Questions about employment also belong with the host route and the host's own terms, not the Scholar designation.

A field or mission to explore

If you are early in your experience or still comparing options, start with a field-based program such as Humanity, Science, Civic, Education, Earth, Innovation, International Graduate, or National Capacity. Senior and Distinguished routes are intended for established records and leadership.

Career Advancement Track

Professional growth aligned with public purpose

The Career Advancement Track helps national and international graduates pursue field-aligned public-interest appointments with qualified host organizations, research institutes, universities, and mission-driven institutions.

It supports fellowship identity, host affiliation, project definition, public-interest contribution, and mission alignment.

Designed for alignment

  • Academic or professional field alignment
  • Defined public-benefit contribution
  • Qualified Host Institution affiliation
  • Clear project and mission scope
  • Membership in the Fellowship Society
International Graduate Fellows

Global knowledge in service to American institutions

International Graduate Fellows are selected for academic achievement, field alignment, service orientation, and commitment to public-interest work. Through qualified hosts, they may contribute to research, civic projects, science, education, environmental work, humanitarian missions, and other areas of public consequence.

National Capacity Fellows

Expertise for institutional strength

Choosing a pathway

Program fit follows the work

Applicants do not need to force a complex record into a single label. The strongest pathway is the one that best explains the field, mission, level of responsibility, and institutional setting of the proposed contribution.

Start with capability

Identify the knowledge, craft, leadership, research practice, or lived professional experience you can apply with confidence.

Name the mission

Connect that capability to a public-interest question you understand well enough to approach with humility and discipline.

Define the setting

Consider the host, collaborators, supervision, timeframe, and access required for the work to be responsible and useful.

What varies

Appointments are not one-size-fits-all

Scope, duration, cadence, location, support, and outputs may differ by program and host. Those terms are made explicit for an individual appointment rather than implied by the fellowship name alone.

What remains constant

A shared standard across programs

Every pathway joins merit to public purpose, expects responsible conduct, and asks the Fellow to produce work that a mission-driven institution or community can genuinely use.
