Education Fellows · Career Mentorship Track

Helping others advance, from internship to fellowship

The Career Mentorship track is a named track inside the Education fellowship program. It is the formal path that the Ignite Curiosity Candidate Placement Internship grows into, entered through the Career Advancement Track.

The mission

Mentorship in service of human potential

Education Fellows serve learning, youth opportunity, mentorship, and human potential. The Career Mentorship track applies that mission to careers: its work is helping people advance their careers, including mentoring the interns who come after.

The pathway has two stages. The beginner stage is the Candidate Placement Internship, an Ignite Curiosity programme within the SPARK Learning System™, taught with the SMILE Method. The formal stage is this fellowship track under US Fellows.

Like the Career Advancement Track, it is not generic placement. The track is defined around mentorship and career development, never as staffing.

Two stages02

An Ignite Curiosity internship builds the craft. The Education Fellows: Career Mentorship track gives it a formal path under US Fellows.

The host institution

Host-based, under the US Fellows appointment model

As with every US Fellows appointment, a Fellow on this track serves with a qualified host. The host provides context, supervision, feedback, and a setting in which people's careers and private information are handled with care. Host requirements and appointment terms remain separate from recognition as a Fellow.

The defined contribution

Four dimensions of the work

Each appointment names the contribution the Fellow will make and the benefit it should leave behind.

01

Career development

Help people understand where their experience fits and take the next step in their careers with confidence.

02

Preparation

Prepare people for the conversations and training that a new role requires.

03

Mentoring new interns

Pass the craft on to those starting at the internship stage, as the Fellow once learned it.

04

Honesty and discretion

Keep private information private and treat every career with honesty and care.

Who it is for

From internship to fellowship

The track is for people who have completed the Candidate Placement Internship, or whose field and record meet what US Fellows' own criteria require. The move usually comes after graduation. Selection is made by US Fellows, and the internship does not guarantee a fellowship.

Learn the craft

Begin with the Candidate Placement Internship at Ignite Curiosity, taught through Seek, Play, Apply, Research, and Know.

Complete the internship

Every skill is signed off by a mentor, one part of the work is taught to a newer intern, and the intern writes their own placement playbook.

Express interest

Apply through the Career Advancement Track, choosing Education Fellows, and describe your field, record, and the contribution you are ready to make.

Serve and mentor

Serve with a qualified host, participate in Society life, and mentor those who follow.

“The internship builds the craft. The fellowship gives it a formal path.”

Take the next step

Express interest through the fellowship application and choose Education Fellows. If you are just starting, begin with the internship at Ignite Curiosity.
