(2026-11-23, owner-authored US-Council/usfellows#1) The International R&D Scholars Program, `international-rd-scholars.html`, is a research-to-career and funding-literacy program for promising international researchers and graduate-level candidates, often with a master's background. It has two parts: a six-month discovery and readiness sprint (research themes and thesis direction, three to five university targets, a faculty map of possible advisor, PI or key-personnel candidates under university rules, funding scans of Grants.gov, SAM.gov, NSF, NASA, USDA and similar sources, funding opportunity snapshots, a concept note or proposal snapshot, recommendations, and an application packet for January and other major PhD deadlines), and a support model of up to ten years (years 0-1 readiness and applications; 1-5 PhD study, assistantships where offered, professor-led research; 3-5 proposal and contract literacy, partners, publications; 5-7 postdoc, university, NGO or industry research options; 7-10 R&D leadership and career choice). Its method is University + Professor + Funding + Partner. Participation in US Fellows stays voluntary and self-directed. It is not a visa pathway or placement: F-1, OPT and work authorization appear only in its boundary statements (no guarantee of admission, funding, professor participation, employment or immigration outcomes; F-1 is a non-immigrant student classification; any work must be authorized; not legal advice or immigration representation). It never says "forever home" or implies permanent residence, and it names no scholar, university, partner, statistic or outcome; its example is abstract.
